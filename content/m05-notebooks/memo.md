title: "Memo 5: Notebook or agent?"
---

Write **half a page**.

### The question

For your team's Third Brain, will you start with a notebook, an agent or both,
and why?

### Template

- **Who will use it** and how often.
- **What sources** it needs, and where they live today (personal or shared).
- **Licences in the team:** what that means for sharing, and for anyone on Chat only.
- **Your choice and the trigger** that would make you change it.
