title: "Memo 4: Our handover standard"
---

Write **half a page or less**. Keep it generic, or use the fictional Coral Ridge context. No real project names, people or company data in the app.

## The question

What should every handover in your team include, who checks it, and how will you know it worked?

## Template

1. **When a handover is required:** stream change, departure, or leave longer than [X] weeks.
2. **The template:** the seven sections, plus anything specific to your department.
3. **The prompt:** your adapted Steps 1 to 4 from lesson 3.
4. **Who checks the commitments section,** and by when.
5. **The watch-outs:** who writes them, and where they are recorded.
6. **The one-month follow-up question:** "What did the pack miss?"
7. **Where the pack is stored,** and who can see it.
