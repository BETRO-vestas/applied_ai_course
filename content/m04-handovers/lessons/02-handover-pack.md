## The one idea to keep

A handover pack has a fixed structure, so the incoming lead knows where to look
and so you can check that nothing has been left out. Six sections can be drafted
with Copilot. The seventh must be written by a person.

## Seven sections

| # | Section | What goes in it | Main source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Project snapshot** | Stage, next milestones, health in one line | Plans, status reports |
| 2 | **Decisions in force** | Current decisions, with history where reversed | Decision log, plus later emails and meetings |
| 3 | **Commitments** | What we promised, to whom, by when, and status | Emails, chats, meeting actions |
| 4 | **Open items and risks** | Unresolved questions, open interfaces, disputes | Registers, emails, vendor notes |
| 5 | **People** | Who is who now (customer, operator, internal) and how they like to work | Contacts list, latest emails |
| 6 | **Where things are** | Links to key documents, latest revisions only | File locations |
| 7 | **Watch-outs** | Judgement: sensitivities, history, what the customer cares about | **The outgoing lead** |

## Every line needs a source

Every line in sections 2 to 6 should link to where it came from. That gives the
incoming lead a way to check the line and a way to read the detail later. A line
with no source is an opinion, and the incoming lead should treat it as one.

## Status words that mean something

Agree a small set of status words and use them the same way every time:

**Open, Done, Superseded, At risk, Not stated.**

"Superseded" matters most. It stops the incoming lead acting on a decision that
has already changed. When a later source changes an earlier one, show both
entries, mark the earlier one Superseded, and cite the later source.

## Where a pack goes wrong

- **Decisions** are copied from the decision log, which was not updated after a
  later email reversed the decision.
- **Commitments** are missing because they were made in chat, not in a meeting.
- **People** are out of date because the contacts list was not changed when a
  customer contact moved on.
- **Watch-outs** are left blank, or filled with Copilot's guesses.
