---
title: "In your department"
---

| Department | Typical handover | What gets lost |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical design | Design to construction | Conditions on design approvals; deviations agreed by email |
| SCADA and cyber | Project to operations | Security exceptions and their expiry dates |
| Siting | Assessment to project | Why a layout or assumption was chosen |
| Product integrity | Investigation to fleet action | Hypotheses already ruled out |
| Lifecycle engineering | Offer to execution | Scope boundaries promised to the customer |
| Technical sales | Bid to project | Assumptions, exclusions and side promises |
